> what does this mean?                                                          
                                                                                
  bucketed into a few point bands instead of raw continuous values (avoids      
  overfitting to noise),                                                        

● It means: instead of using the exact raw number (e.g. "monthly income =
  $1,847.32"), we group values into a few ranges — like income quartiles — and
  assign each range a fixed point value (e.g. top 25% of customers → 100 pts,
  bottom 25% → 10 pts).

  Why: a raw number can look meaningfully different between two customers
  ($1,847 vs $1,850) when really that difference is just noise — random
  variation that doesn't actually predict repayment ability. If the model
  treated every tiny difference in the raw number as meaningful, it would be
  "fitting" to that noise instead of the real signal (that both customers are,
  say, mid-income). Bucketing into bands smooths that out — customers in the
  same band get treated the same, which is more robust and closer to how a human
  underwriter would actually judge "is this income good, okay, or low."

  That's exactly what your NTILE(4) quartile scoring in scripts/02–05 does — it
  turns a continuous ratio (income, DTI, spend-to-income, etc.) into 4 discrete
  bands rather than scoring the raw decimal value directly.
